Privacy Policy

Who handles your information

VYNTRIO Agency provides this website and private project workspace. Contact vyntrioagency@gmail.com for privacy questions, access or correction requests, consent withdrawal, deletion requests, or grievances.

What we collect and why

When you submit an inquiry, we collect your name, email, phone number, project type, requirements and contact consent to review your idea and respond. For agreed projects, we process account identifiers, messages, files, milestones, quotations, invoices and payment references to deliver the service and maintain records. Please do not upload passwords, government identity documents or bank credentials.

Payments and service providers

Cashfree processes checkout and payment details; VYNTRIO stores order references, amounts and verified payment status rather than full card details. Vercel hosts the application. Supabase provides the database, authentication and private file storage. Google services deliver email and hold private encrypted backup copies. Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent automated abuse. These providers process information under their own terms, and processing may occur outside India.

Cookies and optional analytics

Essential session cookies support sign-in and security. The cookie policy explains optional analytics. Google Analytics is not loaded until configured and you choose Allow analytics; you can change your choice using Cookie preferences. We do not send your project details, email, phone number or sign-in tokens as analytics events.

Retention and deletion

Inquiry and project records are retained while needed to respond, perform agreed work, maintain required business records or address disputes. You can ask us to review retention or delete information that is no longer required. Encrypted database snapshots are retained for 30 days; separate file copies may remain until an approved deletion is completed. Removing a current record does not immediately remove it from an existing backup.

Your choices and requests

You can ask for information about processing, correction, erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent, or grievance review by emailing us from the address associated with your inquiry. We may verify ownership before releasing or changing records. Withdrawing necessary contact consent may prevent us from continuing an inquiry. If unresolved, you may use the applicable statutory grievance process.

Security and children

Access controls, staff two-step verification and encrypted backup copies help protect records, but no online service can promise absolute security. This service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to contact us before providing personal information.

Policy changes

We will update this page when our processing changes. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
