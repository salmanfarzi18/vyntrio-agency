Refund Policy

Inquiry confirmation fee

The ₹1 payment confirms your interest in discussing a project. It is separate from project development charges and does not guarantee acceptance or a consultation.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited but confirmation is pending, do not pay again immediately. Contact vyntrioagency@gmail.com with your inquiry reference, payment reference, date and amount. Do not send card numbers, PINs or OTPs. We check the payment provider’s record before confirming a payment or assessing a refund request.

Refund requests

Contact us for a refund review if you believe a payment was duplicated, incorrectly collected or otherwise requires correction. Eligibility is assessed against the payment record, applicable law and any separately agreed project terms. Approved refunds return through the original payment method where supported. Bank and provider processing times vary; we do not promise instant credit.

Project payments

Refunds, cancellations and milestone charges for a development project follow its written agreement and applicable law. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
