Cookie Policy

Essential functions

The private workspace uses session cookies to sign you in and protect access. Spam prevention may use Cloudflare security storage. These functions are needed to provide the requested service.

Optional analytics

If Google Analytics has been configured, it loads only after you select Allow analytics. It helps measure public page visits and confirmed inquiry submissions. We do not use advertising features, session recording or analytics on management, authentication and private workspace pages.

Your controls

You can allow or reject optional analytics using Cookie preferences on the public website. Your choice is stored on your device. Withdrawal stops analytics on subsequent loading and clears accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. Browser settings can block or delete cookies, but blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in.
